Vendored from the astro_data repository (hor-tools), commit fe6fd93.
Keep this copy unchanged so it can be updated from upstream; Morinus-specific glue lives in traditional.py and swe_compat.py.

Local change (not yet upstream): analysis/solar.py and almuten.py handle polar day and night,
when the Sun does not rise or set: sunrise/sunset are None and there is no
planetary hour ruler (before, the analysis failed for such charts).

Also local: comments, docstrings and a few printed texts are reworded to name
no source material, and two star-table constants are renamed CATALOGUE_STARS
and FIRST_MAGNITUDE_PRIMARY_NATURE (no change in behaviour).

Local changes from the final audit (November 2026):
- models.ChartInput has an optional `sect` ("day"/"night"); analysis/sect.chart_sect
  and analysis/solar.solar_frame_for_chart use it when given, so every table
  takes the one sect Morinus decides (its day/night orb, or the user's answer).
- analysis/technical.build_natal_technical_report takes `almuten_override`: the
  temperament uses Morinus's own Almuten Figuris (Tables > Almutens and its
  options) instead of this package's.
- analysis/temperament: the Sun has the quality of its season and the Moon of
  its phase; a planet in House I is not counted again as an
  aspect to the Ascendant; ASC_CONTACT_ORB is 6 (the host sets it and
  MOON_CONTACT_ORB from its options).
- dignity.MEAN_SPEED is replaced in place by the host (traditional.py): the
  traditional mean daily motions by default, these astronomical ones as an option.
